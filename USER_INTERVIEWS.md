# User Interviews

## Interview 1

Role:
Frontend Developer

Pain Point:
Using multiple AI subscriptions without tracking actual usage efficiency.

Feedback:
Would like centralized visibility into AI tooling costs.

---

## Interview 2

Role:
Startup Founder

Pain Point:
Team members independently purchasing AI subscriptions.

Feedback:
Interested in consolidated billing insights.

---

## Interview 3

Role:
Engineering Student

Pain Point:
Unsure which AI plans provide best value.

Feedback:
Would like recommendation-based optimization suggestions.

---

# Key Insights

- Users often overpay unknowingly
- Teams lack centralized AI spend visibility
- Optimization recommendations are valuable
- Simplicity is preferred over complex analytics
